Reading the study

  • The mascot is rasterised from peacock.ts — same traced geometry, stamped dot by dot. Nothing is redrawn.
  • Caller text 23px / 1.42 · reply 22px · metadata 9px pixel caps. Tap targets stay at 44px.
  • Every ratio is measured: text on its own ground clears 8.6:1; reply text on the accent block clears 7.3:1.
  • Drop-in mapping: --field→--paper, --panel→--surface, --accent→--primary, --signal→--line.
